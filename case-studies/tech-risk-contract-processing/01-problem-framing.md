# 01. Problem Framing

## The problem
*(What is broken today? Be specific — e.g. "Tech Risk analysts manually review every vendor/outsourcing contract clause-by-clause against regulatory frameworks, which takes X hours per contract and creates a review backlog.")*

## Who feels this pain
*(Which team, which role, how often — e.g. Technology Risk analysts, 2nd-line Compliance reviewers)*

## Why now
*(What makes this the right problem to solve now — increasing vendor/SaaS contract volume, regulatory scrutiny, cost pressure, etc.)*

## Cost of inaction
*(What happens if this isn't solved — missed risk clauses, slower vendor onboarding, audit findings, analyst burnout)*

## Non-goals
*(Explicitly state what this case study is NOT trying to solve — e.g. not replacing legal review, not making autonomous approval decisions)*

## Hypothesis
*(One sentence: "If we [do X], then [outcome Y], because [reasoning].")*
